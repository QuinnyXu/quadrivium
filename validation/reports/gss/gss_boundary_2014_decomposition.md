# GSS 2014 frame expansion — RH clause-(b) boundary decomposition

**Boundary:** FY2013 → FY2014. **Method:** fixed-cohort decomposition on all-grad
(FT+PT) total enrollment. **Date:** 2026-06-02. Surfaced empirically (HD 4.1 gate).

## The discontinuity

Total enrollment rises **633,010 → 666,586 (net +33,576, +5.3%)** and the surveyed
institution count jumps **576 → 716 (+140 net)** — a one-year **frame expansion**
combined with genuine growth. Unlike 1984–87 (frame contraction with flat net) and
2017 (definitional cohort drop), 2014 moves enrollment *and* frame in the same
(positive) direction.

## Decomposition (clause (b))

| Driver | Value | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Fixed-cohort change** (569 institutions both years) | **+19,215** (+3.0%) | genuine real growth |
| **Frame entrants** (+147 institutions) | **+24,982** | mostly small (avg ~170 students) |
| **Frame leavers** (−7 institutions) | **−10,621** | few but **large** (avg ~1,517) |
| Net frame | **+14,361** | |
| Identity | +19,215 + 24,982 − 10,621 = **+33,576** ✓ | = net |

The +33,576 is **~57% real growth (+19,215) and ~43% net frame (+14,361)**. The
frame term is itself asymmetric: 147 small institutions enter (+24,982) while 7
large institutions leave (−10,621).

## Clauses (a) / (b) / (c)

- **(a) Reconstructible.** Each year is usable on its own frame; the harmonized
  panel carries every institution-year at native UNITID.
- **(b) Decomposable.** Real growth (+19,215, fixed cohort), population expansion
  (+24,982, 147 new institutions), and a −10,621 large-leaver term, named and summed
  to the net.
- **(c) Bounded unmeasurable.** The **7 large leavers (−10,621)** are the residual:
  it is not determinable from GSS alone whether these are genuine exits or **UNITID
  reassignments / mergers** (the IPEDS UnitID-vintage problem deferred to IPEDS #4 —
  cf. HD 4.1 Check 4, the `49xxxx`-block codes). If they are reassignments, the true
  frame expansion is larger and the "leaver" loss is spurious. Bound: ≤ 10,621
  (≤ 1.7% of base) of the 2014 step is attributable to this ambiguous large-leaver
  set; the +19,215 real and +24,982 small-entrant growth are unambiguous.

## Cold-reader guidance
FY2014 onward includes ~140 additional (mostly small) institutions vs FY2013;
real cohort growth that year was ~+3%. Institution-level series spanning 2013→2014
should expect new small reporters and a handful of large UNITID discontinuities.
