# GSS 1984–87 frame contraction — RH clause-(b) boundary decomposition

**Boundary:** the mid-1980s survey-frame contraction — FY1983 → FY1984 (onset) and
FY1987 → FY1988 (recovery). **Method:** fixed-cohort decomposition on all-grad
(FT+PT) total enrollment. **Date:** 2026-06-02. This boundary was **surfaced
empirically** (HD 4.1 gate, not in the original grounding).

## The discontinuity

The surveyed institution count **collapses 599 → 408 at FY1984, holds ~410 through
FY1987, then re-expands to 599 at FY1988** — while total enrollment stays nearly
flat (390,432 → 394,670 → … → 424,523). The discontinuity is in **coverage
(institutions), not the headline enrollment total**: ~190 institutions leave the
frame for four years and return.

## Decomposition (clause (b))

**Onset — FY1983 → FY1984** (net **+4,238**, +1.1%):

| Driver | Value | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Fixed-cohort (404 institutions both years) | **+26,724** (+6.8%) | strong underlying growth |
| Frame leavers (−195 institutions) | **−22,989** | ~190 institutions drop out |
| Frame entrants (+4) | +503 | |
| Identity | +26,724 − 22,989 + 503 = **+4,238** ✓ | |

**Recovery — FY1987 → FY1988** (net **+3,026**, +0.7%):

| Driver | Value | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Fixed-cohort (408) | **−22,157** (−5.3%) | apparent decline (reallocation) |
| Frame entrants (+191) | **+25,456** | the ~190 institutions return |
| Identity | −22,157 + 25,456 − 273 = **+3,026** ✓ | |

The near-flat net **masks two large offsetting components** at each step: a frame
movement of ±~23–25k against a fixed-cohort movement of the opposite sign. The
symmetry (195 leave, 191 return; −22,989 out, +25,456 in) identifies a **frame /
reporting-unit event**, not a real enrollment shock.

## Clauses (a) / (b) / (c)

- **(a) Reconstructible.** Each year is usable on its own institution frame; the
  national enrollment total is continuous across the episode (no headline break).
- **(b) Decomposable.** The episode is a population/frame contraction-then-expansion
  of ~190 institutions, quantified above; the cohort and frame terms are named and
  sum to the (small) net at each step.
- **(c) Bounded unmeasurable.** The fixed-cohort swings (+26,724 at onset, −22,157
  at recovery) are **larger than plausible real enrollment change** and are partly a
  **reporting-unit reallocation** — when institutions leave/return, students shift
  between the reduced-frame "core" reporters and the departing/returning units
  (e.g. multi-campus consolidation under one reporter 1984–87). Real growth and
  reallocation cannot be separated within the contracted frame. Bound: the headline
  enrollment total (frame-independent) is reliable across 1983–1988; **institution-
  level and per-institution series should treat FY1984–1987 as a reduced-coverage
  window** (the spine count-weighted match dips to ~93% here vs ~99% elsewhere — the
  departing institutions are disproportionately non-HERD/FedSupport units).

## Cold-reader guidance
The national enrollment series is safe across this episode. For institution-level
or coverage-sensitive work, flag FY1984–1987 as a ~410-institution reduced frame
(vs ~600 before/after); do not read the institution-count drop as real attrition.
