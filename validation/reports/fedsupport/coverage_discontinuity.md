> **Correction (universe artifact).** The Phase-2 scope memo's (f) reported ~65% dollar coverage in 1971–1998 with a cliff at 1998/99. That was a **universe artifact**: the probe included **Academic FFRDC** rows (a separate institution type carrying the `na_ffrdc` sentinel by design) in the denominator. On the **join universe** (academic + consortium, FFRDC excluded — consistent with (b)/(c)), early-era coverage is **~92–97%**, not 65%, and there is no 1998/99 cliff. The FFRDC reporting boundary is real but belongs to the FFRDC universe (§3 below), not the higher-ed join. Caught by independent recompute; logged as a calibration finding in `seeds/overrides.md`.

## 2. The genuine join-universe steps (decomposed, four-driver template)

Three modest steps, NOT a 34-point early cliff: **1993→94** (~92→96%, up), **2008→09** and **2015→16** (down). The two down-steps decompose as:

**2008→2009: 97.0% → 93.5% dollar coverage.** Unmatched $ rose $846,244K → $2,340,460K (**+$1,494,216K**), which partitions **additively** into three terms:  
  - population expansion (new institutions absent the prior year): **$1,294K**;  
  - matched→unmatched recode across 2 continuing institutions (back-assignment vintage): **$930K**;  
  - dollars into institutions ALREADY (partly) `no_match` — i.e. real-growth flowing onto pre-existing system-level no-match rows: **$1,491,992K**.  
  (The three sum to the +$1,494,216K increase by construction.)  
**2015→2016: 93.3% → 89.5% dollar coverage.** Unmatched $ rose $2,058,652K → $3,313,570K (**+$1,254,918K**), which partitions **additively** into three terms:  
  - population expansion (new institutions absent the prior year): **$4,259K**;  
  - matched→unmatched recode across 21 continuing institutions (back-assignment vintage): **$1,228,177K**;  
  - dollars into institutions ALREADY (partly) `no_match` — i.e. real-growth flowing onto pre-existing system-level no-match rows: **$22,482K**.  
  (The three sum to the +$1,254,918K increase by construction.)  

- **2008→09** is dominated by the **third term ($1,491,992K)** — **real growth (ARRA, $28.6B→$35.9B) flowing into pre-existing system-level `no_match` attributions** (U. Michigan, Pittsburgh, Rutgers — Seam-B system rows), NOT new recipients (population expansion is $1,294K of the $1,494,216K). The reviewer's ARRA-new-recipient hypothesis is **ruled out**; ARRA is a dollar amplifier of the existing Seam-B no-match set.
- **2015→16** is dominated by the **second term ($1,228,177K, 21 institutions)** — a **back-assignment vintage**: continuing institutions recoded matched→unmatched, **Johns Hopkins alone (~$1.1B) ≈ 87% of the step**. Population expansion ~0.
- **Neither step is population expansion**; both are vintage / Seam-B amplification on continuing institutions. This mirrors the HERD 2008→2011 four-driver decomposition (real growth vs definitional vs population vs residual): here real growth (ARRA) and back-assignment vintage are the live drivers; population is nil.

## 3. FFRDCs — a SEPARATE universe (the relocated finding)

Academic FFRDC and Nonprofit FFRDC are present **FY1971–FY1998** only, then absent — this is the real **FFRDC reporting boundary** (the survey stopped attributing FFRDC obligations to academic administrators after FY1998). In FY1971 Academic FFRDC was **29.6%** of (academic+consortium+Academic-FFRDC) dollars, declining to ~19% by FY1998, then 0. FFRDCs carry the `na_ffrdc` UNITID sentinel by design (a national lab is not an IPEDS institution) and are **excluded from the HERD join universe**. This is an RH clause-(b) object **in the FFRDC universe** — a real structural break, relocated to the correct universe, NOT a higher-ed coverage gap.
